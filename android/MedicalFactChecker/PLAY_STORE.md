## Full description (4000 characters max)
```
Medical Fact Checker tells you what the published research says about a health claim. Type a statement such as "Intermittent fasting lowers blood pressure", and the app searches the medical literature, reads the studies it finds and writes an evidence report with a verdict and citations you can check yourself.

HOW A CLAIM IS CHECKED
• Search: your claim becomes literature searches in PubMed, Europe PMC (which includes preprints), or both.
• Score: an AI model rates each study for how directly it addresses your claim, so weak matches drop out. On-device similarity scoring can be added for a second opinion.
• Read: the app fetches each study's full text from open-access sources wherever it can, or reads a PDF you upload.
• Cite: it pulls out the exact passages that support or contradict the claim, each linked to its paper.
• Report: you get a verdict with the reasoning, the citations and the studies behind it, ready to export as a PDF.

EVIDENCE YOU CAN LOOK BEHIND
• Retraction checks: every paper is checked against Crossref and the Retraction Watch database. Retracted papers are left out of the verdict and named in the report; papers under an expression of concern are flagged.
• Transparency ratings: each paper is rated on its funding, conflict-of-interest, data-availability and trial-registration statements, so you can see who paid for the research.
• Full text first: open-access full text is gathered from PubMed Central, Europe PMC, Unpaywall and OpenAlex, and from CORE and Elsevier with your own free API keys. Long articles are read in full, never cut short.
• Every reference opens the original article.

YOUR CHOICE OF AI
Use your own API key for Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, DeepSeek, Groq or Mistral AI, or run open models on your own computer with Ollama and keep the analysis off the cloud entirely. You pay your provider directly, and the app shows what each run costs. Set a limit per run and per month so a check never costs more than you expect.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-up.
• No analytics, no advertising and no trackers.
• Your claims, reports and history stay on your phone. API keys are stored encrypted.
• Searches go only to the public research databases, and the articles' text goes only to the AI provider you choose.
• Optional sync writes your history to a folder you pick, so you decide where it goes.

WHO IT IS FOR
• Doctors, nurses and other health professionals who want a quick look at the literature
• Researchers and students learning to weigh medical evidence
• Journalists and writers checking a health claim before they repeat it
• Anyone who wants to know what the science behind a headline really says

WHAT YOU NEED
An API key from one of the supported AI providers, or an Ollama server you run yourself. An optional free NCBI API key makes PubMed searches faster.

OPEN SOURCE
Medical Fact Checker is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License. The source code is on GitHub at github.com/hherb/bmlibrarian_lite.

IMPORTANT
Medical Fact Checker is a research tool that summarises published studies. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. AI-generated summaries can contain errors, so check important findings in the original papers, and talk to a doctor or other qualified health professional before making decisions about your health.

Website and privacy policy: medfactcheck.org
```
